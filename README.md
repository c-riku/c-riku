## Hi, I'm Caius 👋

Data & AI Developer in Helsinki, Finland, currently Data Developer at [Aitomation](https://aitomation.fi/). I build **data pipelines**, **Power BI reporting** and **AI agents** that people use in their daily work, working directly with stakeholders from scoping to production.

🌐 **Portfolio:** [caiusribeiro.github.io](https://caiusribeiro.github.io/) · short link [iki.fi/caius.ribeiro](https://iki.fi/caius.ribeiro)

### What I do
I combine data engineering, BI and AI automation, so a solution can go from the source system all the way to a report or an agent without changing hands.

- **Data engineering:** Python and SQL pipelines that bring data from APIs and business systems into a layered warehouse, with automated loading, data-quality checks and infrastructure as code.
- **BI & reporting:** Power BI models and reports that management actually uses, from financial statements and P&L reconciliation to sales and resourcing KPIs, with source control from test to production.
- **AI agents & automation:** Copilot Studio agents and Power Automate flows that fit into the tools people already use, grounded in the right knowledge and with a person in the approval loop.
